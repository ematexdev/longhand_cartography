How a commission is made

Six stages, no shortcuts

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A conversation — thirty minutes, usually — about the place, the memory attached to it, and the one detail that has to appear no matter what.

  2. 02

    Reference Gathering

    We collect your photos, GPS tracks, and half-remembered stories, so the smallest waypoint still lands in the right place.

  3. 03

    The Longhand Draft

    Every coastline, ridge, and street is drawn by hand with dip pen and iron-gall ink on cotton rag paper. Nothing is traced from a screen.

  4. 04

    Proofing

    A high-resolution scan comes to you for notes before a single line is inked twice. Most maps need one round.

  5. 05

    Digitization

    The finished draft is scanned at archival resolution and prepared for giclée print, colour-matched to the original ink.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Your map arrives rolled in a rigid tube, ready to frame. The original hand-drawn draft is available as an add-on.

Instruments

Every map begins as paper, not pixels.

Paper
300 gsm cotton rag, the same stock used for archival etching — it takes ink without feathering and holds up for a hundred years framed.
Ink
Iron-gall for line work, walnut ink for wash and shading. Both darken slightly with age, the way a good map should.
Framing
Raw oak, blackened ash, or unframed and rolled — three options, museum glass on all of them.

Questions we're asked most

How long does a commission take?

Typically 6–10 weeks from consultation to delivery, depending on how much reference gathering the place needs.

Do you need GPS data, or can you work from a description?

Either works. The richest maps usually mix both — memory for texture, GPS for accuracy.

Do you ship outside the studio's country?

Yes. Every map ships insured, rolled in a rigid tube, worldwide.

What sizes do you offer?

Three standard sizes, plus one bespoke wall-scale size for business and expedition commissions.

Is the original hand-drawn draft included?

The print is included in every commission. The original ink draft is available as a paid add-on, while it lasts — only one exists per map.
