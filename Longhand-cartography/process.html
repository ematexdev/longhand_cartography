Our workflow

Six careful stages from initial conversation to your finished map.

We move deliberately: no rushed drafts, no tracing from screens, and one proof before the ink is final.

Step by step

How a commission becomes a map.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We talk through your memories, your reference, and the emotional detail that gives the map its soul.

  2. 02

    Reference Gathering

    Photos, GPS tracks, old maps and stories are combined so we can build a map that is intimate and grounded.

  3. 03

    The Longhand Draft

    Every line is made with a dip pen on cotton rag paper. The goal is clarity first, artistry second.

  4. 04

    Proofing

    A high-resolution scan comes to you for review so revisions happen before the final ink is set.

  5. 05

    Digitization

    We scan the finished drawing and prepare it for printing with careful color matching and archival settings.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Your map arrives rolled in a rigid tube, ready to frame. Originals are offered as a limited add-on.
