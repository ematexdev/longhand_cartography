Pricing

Simple starting prices for custom map commissions.

Every map is unique, but our starting points make it easy to understand how commissioning works.

Packages

Three starting points, plus bespoke options.

Studio map
From £1,200 — one hand-drawn map, digitized and delivered as an archival print.
Deluxe map
From £1,800 — larger format, additional detail, and one round of proof revisions.
Wall-scale commission
From £2,900 — a large-format presentation piece for hospitality, expedition, or business spaces.

Custom pricing depends on scale, detail, and whether you want the original draft added to your order. Every commission includes a downloadable print-ready file.

Typical turnaround is 6–10 weeks from the first consultation, with faster delivery available for select projects.
