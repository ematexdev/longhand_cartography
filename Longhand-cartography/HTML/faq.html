FAQ

Helpful answers for commissioning a custom map.

Everything you need to know about timelines, reference, shipping, and revisions.

Questions we hear most

How it works, in practical terms.

How long does a commission take?

Typically 6–10 weeks from consultation to delivery, depending on project size and reference availability.

Do you need GPS data, or can you work from a description?

Either works. The richest maps combine memory and reference with GPS, but we can also build a map from your description and photos.

Do you ship outside the studio's country?

Yes. Every map ships insured and rolled in a rigid tube, worldwide.

What sizes do you offer?

Three standard sizes plus bespoke wall-scale commissions for business and expedition projects.

Is the original hand-drawn draft included?

The print is included with every commission. The original ink draft is available as a limited add-on.

Can I change the map after the proof?

Yes — one round of revisions is included before the final ink stage. After that, additional changes may require a new commission.
