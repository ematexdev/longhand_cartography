Materials

Every map starts with paper, ink, and care.

We choose materials for their permanence and their warmth: archive-grade paper, traditional ink, and giclée printing that honors the original drawing.

Studio tools

Heritage materials for modern commissions.

Paper
300 gsm cotton rag paper chosen for its texture, ink handling, and archival permanence.
Ink
Iron-gall ink for line work and walnut washes for tonal detail. Both deepen with age without bleeding.
Printing
Archival giclée prints matched to the original drawing, with optional framing in oak, ash, or raw linen.
