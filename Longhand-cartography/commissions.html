Commission types

Bespoke maps for the moments you want to keep forever.

From a trail you finished alone to a wedding crossing, each map is made to tell the story of a place or journey.

What we draw

Maps built around every kind of story.

Personal milestone

Journeys and homecomings

Cross-country moves, long walks, and the route from your first home to the place you built next.

Wedding and anniversary

Routes that brought you together

Sailing crossings, first meetups, and the harbour or street where you said yes.

Business and hospitality

Wall-scale maps for reception spaces

Custom maps for an office, restaurant, or briefing room, drawn to feel like a place your guests remember.

Expedition

Routes taken off the beaten path

Climbs, long-distance hikes, and ocean crossings rendered with the subtle details only a traveler remembers.

Edition and study

Private harbour charts and landmark studies

Functional, beautiful drawings for clients who want a piece of the world on their wall.

How it works

Each commission is shaped by the place and the people behind it.

We begin with a conversation, gather your photos and tracks, then hand-draw every line on cotton rag paper. The result is a map that is accurate where it matters and expressive everywhere else.
